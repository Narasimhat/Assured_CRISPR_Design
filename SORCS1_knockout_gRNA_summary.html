Ordering & validation notes

  • Order spacers without PAM; use SpCas9 (NGG).
  • For U6 expression, prepend a leading G if the spacer does not start with G.
  • Sanger-sequence the target locus in your parental line before ordering (SNPs can block cutting).
  • Test 2–4 guides; dual-guide RNP increases indel rate.
  • Validate KO by ICE/TIDE plus protein loss (western/IF); prefer frameshifting indels.
